### 🚀 About Me

I'm an **Applied AI Engineer at Novo Precision**, building shop-floor production intelligence systems — OPC-UA dashboards, RFID smart workstations, Andon alert systems, and camera-assisted tracking pipelines that turn raw manufacturing data into real-time decisions.

I recently graduated with an **M.S. in Data Science from the University of New Haven** (4.0 GPA, TCoE Endowed Graduate Fellow), where my research centered on **multimodal AI, explainable AI, and safety-critical assistive systems**.

Before that, I was an AI Engineer at **Vuzix Corporation**, where I shipped 8 production AR applications for smart eyewear — including **Guide AI**, a navigation aid for visually impaired users with 200+ active users.

---

### 🔬 Research & Flagship Projects

- **[HazSense](#)** — A manually verified dataset of 2,000+ RGB-D image pairs across 20+ hazard categories, built after discovering 88–90% automated labeling failure rates on safety-critical objects. *(IEEE ICIR 2025)*
- **[HazDepth](#)** — Depth-Anything + Faster R-CNN pipeline for monocular metric depth recovery in hazard detection. *(Springer Nature Applied Intelligence)*
- **Guide AI** — Production AR navigation system for visually impaired users, built at Vuzix, 200+ active users.
- **EduPredict Pro** — ARIMA-based enrollment forecasting + decision intelligence dashboard for university deans. *(IEEE ISEC Princeton, ASEE-NE)*
- **Smart Router** — ML-based DoS detection running on Raspberry Pi. *(IEEE UEMCON, presented at IBM HQ)*
- **EcoFlight AI** — 🏆 1st place, Hack New Haven (judged by Sikorsky engineers)
